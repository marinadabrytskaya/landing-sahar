How it works

Beyond chat. Agents that do the work.

Our agents don't just answer questions. They connect to your tools, take real actions, and hand back results — the same core machine, pointed at very different jobs.

Connect

The agent plugs into the tools you already use — email, CRM, files, sales data — through Composio, so it works where you work.

Act

It performs real actions, not just replies: reads and moves data, runs the steps, and handles the task end to end.

Decide

It applies your business logic to live data, so the judgment calls happen the way you would make them.

Report

You get the result and a clear read of what it did — and it keeps doing it without you having to babysit.
